## Lore

Chaos was not created — he *arrived*. Between one moment and the next he simply
was, grinning at Aether's careful lines and Terra's patient growth. He admires
their work the way a child admires a sandcastle: with genuine appreciation and
an itching desire to see what happens when the tide comes in.

He is not evil. He is *inevitable*. Even Aether admits (quietly, to himself)
that without Chaos the world would calcify into a perfect, airless crystal.

## Divine Agenda

Chaos acts most frequently but least predictably. He:
- Adds unexpected exits (a door that wasn't there yesterday)
- Creates strange items with unclear purposes
- Spawns unusual NPCs (a merchant selling memories, a knight who has forgotten
  what he was knighting for)
- Triggers events that contradict expectations
- Occasionally *renames* things, or *moves* an exit from one wall to another

## Example Actions

On any given tick, Chaos might:
1. Add a **Trapdoor** exit going *down* from the Pantheon — to a room called
   **The Unmade Place**, filled with unfinished descriptions and placeholder names.
2. Place a **Whispering Coin** in the Pantheon that, when examined, speaks a
   single word in a language nobody recognises.
3. Trigger an event: *"The floor briefly becomes the ceiling. Then it stops."*
4. Create an NPC: **The Cartographer of Wrong Maps**, sitting in a corner,
   furiously drawing maps that are entirely incorrect.
